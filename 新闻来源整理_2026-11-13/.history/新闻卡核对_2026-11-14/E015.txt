L6@P1: SUMMARY
L7@P1: Overview of Continuing Appropriations for
L8@P1: FY2026 (Division A of P.L. 119-37)
L9@P1: On November 12, 2025, the President signed into law H.R. 5371—the Continuing
L10@P1: Appropriations, Agriculture, Legislative Branch, Military Construction and Veterans Affairs, and
L11@P1: Extensions Act, 2026—as P.L. 119-37. Division A of the act—the Continuing Appropriations
L12@P1: Act, 2026—provides continuing appropriations through January 30, 2026, for agencies funded
L13@P1: through nine of the 12 regular appropriations bills. Divisions B-D of the act each include one
L14@P1: regular, full-year appropriations act for FY2026, and Divisions E-H include various authorization
L15@P1: extensions and other legislative provisions. This report examines only Division A, the
L16@P1: “continuing resolution” (CR) portion of the act.
L17@P1: The enactment of P.L. 119-37 ended a 42-day lapse in appropriations (a “funding gap”) lasting from October 1, 2025,
L18@P1: through November 11, 2025. As none of the regular bills had been enacted prior to the start of FY2026, agencies funded
L19@P1: through all 12 regular appropriations bills were required to cease operations of affected activities (a “government shutdown”),
L20@P1: except in certain situations when law authorizes continued activity.
L21@P1: For most programs, projects, and activities, the CR provides funding from November 12, 2025, through January 30, 2026 (an
L22@P1: 80-day, or roughly 11.5-week, period) for agencies to continue operating at the rates, authorities, and conditions as provided
L23@P1: for in the Full-Year Continuing Appropriations Act, 2025 (Division A of P.L. 119-4). The Congressional Budget Office
L24@P1: (CBO) estimates that the CR provides an annualized amount of $1.560 trillion in discretionary budget authority for FY2026.
L25@P1: In the event that one or more regular appropriations bills for FY2026, or an additional CR, are not enacted prior to the
L26@P1: expiration of this CR, then another funding gap would occur beginning on January 31, 2026, that could result in a shutdown
L27@P1: of most affected activities.
L28@P1: The CR includes several provisions related to the period of the funding gap. The act specifies that it provides coverage for the
L29@P1: entire period of the funding gap beginning on October 1, covers certain obligations incurred during it and provides for
L30@P1: reimbursements to states or other federal grantees for costs incurred during the gap that would have otherwise been paid by
L31@P1: the federal government. The CR also provides for backpay for federal workers for the period of the funding gap, reverses
L32@P1: reductions in force (RIFs) initiated during it, and prohibits certain RIFs from being initiated for the duration of the CR.
L33@P1: As has been typical with CRs, Division A of P.L. 119-37 includes several provisions that are specific to certain agencies,
L34@P1: accounts, or programs. These include provisions—known as “anomalies”—that establish exceptions to the general funding
L35@P1: and other provisions of the CR for certain accounts or activities, as well as provisions related to amending or extending
L36@P1: existing law. The section of this report titled “Agency-, Account-, and Program-Specific Provisions” summarizes each of
L37@P1: these provisions included in the CR.
